# Code of Conduct

Dropwheel follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and
publishing anyone's private information without their permission are not acceptable, here or
in any space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
([@dogutez](https://github.com/dogutez)). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove comments, close discussions or block people who don't follow these rules.
